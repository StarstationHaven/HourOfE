We welcome **Alonzo Brinson** from Lake City, Florida, and a senior at Morehouse College in Atlanta, Georgia.

Alonzo began preaching at just **16 years old** and continues to inspire others through faith, leadership, and service. He serves as **SGA Chaplain at Morehouse College** and provides leadership in outreach and evangelism through the **Martin Luther King Jr. International Chapel**.

From mentoring his peers to serving communities throughout Florida and Georgia, Alonzo demonstrates what it means for young people to **lead with purpose and serve with compassion.**
