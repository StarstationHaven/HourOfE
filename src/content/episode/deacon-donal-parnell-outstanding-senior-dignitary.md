Born on **August 15, 1923**, Deacon Parnell is 103 years old and proud of a lifetime spent working and providing for his family. A lifelong farmer, he cultivated crops including **tobacco, corn, and cotton**. Throughout his working years, he was employed at **Aero** and worked as a **service station employee**.

Family is one of Deacon Parnell's greatest sources of pride. He is the father of **five children** and grandfather of **six grandchildren**, all of whom he proudly describes as successful. Among his grandchildren are accomplished professionals and community leaders, including a **lawyer, a doctor, and Columbia County Commissioner District One, Kevin Parnell**.

Longevity also runs strong in the Parnell family. Deacon Parnell has two siblings: **Ms. Joyce Tunsil, age 99, and Ms. Veronica Pinkston, age 100**. His mother lived to be **103 years and three months**, and Deacon Parnell says with confidence that he expects to live even longer!

Join the **Hour of Excellence Podcast** as we celebrate Deacon Donal Parnell's extraordinary journey and hear about a life built on **faith, family, hard work, and a legacy of excellence**.

**At 103 years young, Deacon Donal Parnell continues to inspire us all!**
